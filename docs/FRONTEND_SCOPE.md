# Operational frontend scope

Updated 11 October 2026. The user selected the Seed & Star logo and asked for a less boxy, less crowded interface. The exact supplied PNG now appears in the sidebar, loading screen and mobile header. Forest-black, ivory and sage colors accompany unboxed metrics and open form, review, harvest and device sections. The main overview chart retains one quiet surface. Desktop padding, section spacing and control sizes have increased. The professional operations structure remains informed by official Grafana, Priva Operator, Carbon and ThingsBoard patterns; the [research note](designs/2026-10-11/operations/RESEARCH.md) records those sources. The slogan, decorative tray illustration and journey tiles remain removed.

The latest product-experience revision adds useful exploration patterns researched from award-winning Flighty, Crouton and Lumy, plus Priva, Grafana, OneSoil and Linear primary documentation. See [product research](designs/2026-10-11/product-experience/RESEARCH.md) for exact award evidence and the limits of those design inferences. The selected logo and space background are preserved.

- Six primary groups remain: **Mission overview**, **Crew & nutrition**, **Crops & food**, **What-if simulator**, **Plant screening** and **Live devices**.
- Crew & nutrition has **Nutrition & logs** and **Mission inputs** tabs. Crops & food has **Crop schedules** and **Food inventory** tabs. Unsubmitted mission/inventory drafts and crew intake logs survive navigation; saving inputs invalidates the previous comparison explicitly.
- Secondary **Workspace settings** has **Saved plans** and **Model settings** tabs. Crop references, measured-profile setup, harvest calibration and yield sensitivity sit behind closed disclosures. Crop coefficients sit under **Crop profiles & assumptions**; the USDA table/setup sits under **Food composition & planner setup**. No model, calibration or persistence endpoint was removed, and there is no primary Research page.
- The overview places mission configuration, input provenance, four saved-plan metrics, nutrient coverage and review items above harvest and hardware detail. Adaptive/fixed selection changes metrics, chart, harvest batches and review items together, and carries into the crop schedule workspace. Full-horizon/from-event chart windows read the saved result without solving; whole-run metric scope remains explicit. Empty plans have no invented values. Fixed harvest batches are labelled nominal estimates, separate from realized daily yields.
- The mission-day explorer reads the selected saved daily row. Previous/next controls, a range input and scenario/nominal-harvest/first-target-gap milestones change the selected day, which survives navigation. Open crew, food-reserve, crop and resource nodes show daily context and link to the corresponding workflows. This is simulation context, separate from sensor observations.
- Nutrients, Water and Electricity chart views use saved daily nutrient values and resource-use/available-budget fields. Selected-day and scenario markers provide chart context. Fixed-to-adaptive signed absolute differences compare whole-run target-gap days, harvest and water; they do not establish measured savings or biological benefit.
- A visible navigation search and Ctrl/Cmd K shortcut find ten destinations, including food/crop keywords. Search changes navigation only; it does not search stored records or run a model. Five scenario starters remain editable and require explicit **Run comparison** submission.
- Crop timelines have mission-day endpoints and a keyboard-scrollable bounded view. Existing complete tables and CSV exports remain available.
- Hardware observations poll independently of planning. Hardware and synthetic streams stay separate, missing readings remain unknown, and stale/offline packets or refresh errors cannot present recent hardware status. Receipt freshness does not establish sensor calibration. Outputs remain disabled.
- Prototype badges, demo/mixed input labels, source provenance and relevant nutrition, model and hardware limits remain visible where they affect interpretation.
- The mobile navigation drawer supports keyboard focus, Escape, focus return, and closing on navigation or a return to desktop width. The skip link moves focus into the current workspace without changing routes. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- Existing `#research` bookmarks open Model settings with Workspace settings active. Older nutrition/archive bookmarks remain reachable through their groups; unknown hashes return to the mission overview.

The user subsequently requested a space-like dark background. A CSS star field, faint nebula gradients and five slow twinkles now sit behind the interface. The fixed decorative layers have no pointer events, respect reduced-motion preferences and are hidden when printing. No particle library, JavaScript loop or additional image downloads were introduced. The production build and desktop/phone browser checks passed; captures are under `docs/designs/2026-10-11/space-background/`.
