# Crew-driven food recommendations

Crop schedules opens an automatic advisory report using the accepted mission roster, day, daily nutrient forecasts and remaining resource commitments. There is no required nutrient-priority, area or deadline form. Custom comparison remains under Advanced comparison.

Each crop is an alternative additional batch. Its area is limited by the lowest unallocated area, water and power across its entire growth window, uncommitted finite stores, harvest-day meal capacity and any established nutrient gaps. Tray increments round down. The saved solver's existing future plantings are shown separately. Advice does not allocate crops or alter completed history.

Crew & nutrition shares current food logs and laboratory examples with the report. Complete logs are compared with accepted per-person targets; incomplete or missing logs remain unknown. Draft targets do not overwrite a saved mission and remain visibly pending until applied. Session storage retains the current draft and its sample provenance. Added members do not inherit another member's laboratory values.

Health records are reviewed separately from food forecasts. Applicable low hemoglobin/ferritin reference comparisons request medical review, not an automatic diagnosis or an iron prescription. Current reported chest pain with breathlessness, or fainting, prompts the mission medical response. Historical/future records and fictional samples are distinguished. Heart rate requires a recorded observation, resting context and reviewed comparison limits. No blood-pressure readings or clinical limits currently exist.

Stored-food support uses the saved inventory and expiry status. Supplements are displayed only for a matching person and nutrient with a recorded clinician-approved order, positive available inventory and valid dates; no dose is generated. With no matching order, availability is unconfirmed.

Microorganisms use the saved reviewed biological scenario. The projection starts prospectively, pays startup and operating resources, respects the remaining horizon and reports actual modeled first-harvest timing and protein. Unreviewed modules show missing inputs. Projected biomass is never automatically credited as edible inventory, consumed nutrition or treatment. Crops and microbes must be jointly reviewed before committing shared resources.

Clinical and biological evidence boundaries:

- [NHLBI iron-deficiency anemia](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/anemia/iron-deficiency-anemia): laboratory results and treatment require medical assessment.
- [NHS chest pain](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/chest-pain/): acute associated symptoms require urgent support.
- [NASA BioNutrients](https://www.nasa.gov/general/what-is-bionutrients/): engineered nutrient-production experiments do not establish an available emergency food or general supplement replacement.

Verification covers automatic sizing, conservation of saved commitments/history, current vs future symptoms, fictional provenance, complete vs partial logs, approved supplement inventory and microorganism startup. Visual hosted review remains unverified; no browser review is claimed.
